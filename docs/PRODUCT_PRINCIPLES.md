# Marginalia product principles

## For all the readers

Marginalia exists to help readers tend the books that shape them and preserve
the thoughts that arise along the way.

1. **Private before public.** A reader's library, photographs, and reflections
   are private unless the reader deliberately chooses otherwise.
2. **Reflection before engagement.** Marginalia does not optimize for feeds,
   reactions, streaks, or time on site.
3. **The reader owns the record.** Core data remains exportable in useful,
   human-readable and machine-readable forms.
4. **The copy in hand matters.** A library item represents a reader's own copy,
   not merely a canonical catalogue entry.
5. **Paid craft, not captivity.** Patron features support preservation,
   presentation, storage, and organization. A reader is never charged to
   recover their own words.
6. **Quiet technology.** Infrastructure should be secure, reliable, portable,
   and as invisible to the reader as possible.
7. **Ornament earns its place.** The crest, sacred geometry, shelves, and
   ephemera mark significant moments without competing with reading.
